# 🎮 Retro Games Collection

A collection of interactive browser-based games combining classic gameplay with modern twists!

## 🎯 Games

### 🎮 RPS-15 (Rock Paper Scissors - 15 Gestures)

The ultimate evolution of Rock Paper Scissors! Battle against the computer using 15 unique gestures where each gesture beats exactly 7 others and loses to 7 others.

**Features:**
- ✨ 15 unique gestures: Rock, Fire, Scissors, Snake, Human, Tree, Wolf, Sponge, Paper, Air, Water, Dragon, Devil, Lightning, Gun
- 🎯 Official RPS-15 rules with detailed win/loss relationships
- 📊 Score tracking and statistics (wins, losses, ties, win rate)
- 📜 Game history showing recent rounds
- 🌓 Dark mode toggle
- 🔊 Sound effects with mute option
- ℹ️ Complete gesture information guide
- 📱 Fully responsive design

**How to Play:**
1. Choose one of the 15 gestures
2. Computer randomly selects its gesture
3. See the outcome with a clear explanation (e.g., "Fire burns Paper")
4. Track your performance and improve your strategy!

### 🎄 Christmas Gesture Battle

A unique hybrid game combining Memory Match with RPS-15 battles in a festive Christmas theme!

**Features:**
- 🎁 Memory matching gameplay meets strategic battles
- 🎯 Dual 4×4 boards (player vs AI with different card positions)
- 🔓 Progressive gesture unlocking system
- 🤖 AI with memory (40% chance to remember and match cards)
- ⚔️ Battle phase after each successful match
- 🎨 Festive Christmas theme (red, green, gold colors)
- ✨ Smooth animations and golden glow effects
- 🔔 Jingle bell sound effects
- 🏆 Dual win conditions (first to 8 matches OR highest battle score)
- 📊 Real-time score and progress tracking

**How to Play:**
1. **Match Cards**: Click 2 cards on YOUR board to find matching gesture pairs
2. **Unlock Gestures**: Each successful match unlocks that gesture for battles
3. **Battle Time**: Choose from your unlocked gestures to fight the AI
4. **Strategic Depth**: You and AI unlock different gestures - plan carefully!
5. **Win**: Be first to match all 8 pairs OR have the highest battle score

**Game Rules:**
- Start with 3 unlocked gestures: Rock, Paper, Scissors
- Memory boards contain 8 pairs of OTHER gestures (not the starting 3)
- Turn-based: Make your matches, then AI takes its turn
- AI remembers cards it sees (but not perfectly - keeps it fair!)
- After each match, battle using your unlocked gestures
- Battle winner earns 10 points

## 🎓 About RPS-15

RPS-15 is based on the official game from [umop.com/rps15.htm](http://www.umop.com/rps15.htm). It's a mathematically balanced extension of Rock Paper Scissors where:

- Each gesture beats exactly 7 gestures
- Each gesture loses to exactly 7 gestures
- No ties are more likely than in regular RPS
- Specific action verbs describe each interaction

**Example relationships:**
- Rock **crushes** Scissors, Fire, Snake, Human, Tree, Wolf, Sponge
- Dragon **commands** Fire, **eats** Snake, Wolf, Devil, **breathes fire on** Sponge, Lightning
- Water **erodes** Rock, Fire, Scissors, **drowns** Snake, Human, Tree, **rusts** Gun

## 🎮 Game Strategy Tips

### RPS-15 Strategy:
- Learn the gesture relationships using the info panel
- Watch for patterns in computer choices
- Track your most/least successful gestures
- Try to maintain a balanced win rate across all gestures

### Christmas Battle Strategy:
- Remember card positions on YOUR board
- Unlock powerful gestures early (Dragon, Devil, Lightning, Gun)
- Balance matching speed with battle wins
- AI has memory too - don't assume it plays randomly!
- Plan your battles - you might face gestures you haven't unlocked yet
